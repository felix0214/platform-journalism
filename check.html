    Keep these separate

    Your ID proves.
    Your payment card pays.

    An eligible student needs accepted proof of entitlement and the right payment setup. Your student ID alone does not turn an adult payment into a concession fare.

    $25

    Difference between the adult and concession weekly caps. It is a policy comparison, not a promised weekly saving.

    I have a university scholarship. Is that enough?

    A partial fee discount or a university scholarship does not automatically meet the approved Australian Government funding requirements. Ask your institution to check your award against section 9 of the May 2026 guidelines.

    Can I tap with my phone?

    Eligible concession passengers can register and link an eligible payment card through Transport Connect. Use the same card or device at both ends of the trip and carry accepted proof. Check the official linking steps.

    What if I don’t qualify here?

    This guide covers the tertiary concession pathway only. Your circumstances may fit another category. See all concession categories or ask your institution for help.

    Sources: TfNSW guidelines, Service NSW and contactless guidance.

    About Platform

    A clearer route through student life

    Platform is a solo journalism product prototype for tertiary students in Sydney, with a particular focus on international students navigating unfamiliar public systems.

    This first edition combines an original document-based explainer with a tool that helps readers locate the right official guidance. Platform has no affiliation with UTS, the UTS Students’ Association or Transport for NSW. There is no advertising or sponsored content.

    Reporting uses public documents and attributed, previously published material. No interviews or reader research are claimed. The photograph is an identified archive image. The tool processes answers in your browser and does not send or store them.

    For a possible correction, copy the relevant claim and source from the source register and send it with this prototype to the student author. This prototype has no staffed inbox.
